LeafScope is a controlled study of agricultural self-supervised pretraining for crop-disease
recognition, run on a single laptop GPU.

## What it covers

- **Six crops**, studied under the same controlled protocol.
- **Feature distillation** as part of the pretraining setup.
- **A fast "pre-flight" predictor** of transfer gain.
